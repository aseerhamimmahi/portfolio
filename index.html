Introduction

An introduction, filed from Dhaka

A computer science & engineering student who builds mobile apps, frontend experiences, and database-driven systems — currently finishing a B.Sc. at East West University and shipping side projects in the gaps between lectures, lab reports, and club meetings.

I'm Aseer Hamim Mahi, based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and I've been writing code long enough to have opinions about it. My work sits at the meeting point of three things I genuinely enjoy: the tight feedback loop of a mobile app, the visual precision of a well-built interface, and the quiet satisfaction of a database schema that still makes sense six months later.

Most of what I know came from building things with a deadline attached. A blood-donor matching app in Java and Firebase, because a classroom exercise wasn't enough. A real-time appliance energy dashboard in Node.js and MongoDB, because I wanted to watch live voltage and current numbers move across a screen and understand every layer between the sensor and the pixel. A decision-support tool that scores computer hardware against weighted criteria, a personal finance tracker with budgets and charts, and a WebSocket chat app that exists mostly because I refused to accept that "real-time" was magic.

Alongside the code, I've spent two years coordinating people. As a senior volunteer with the EWU Robotics Club and the EWU Computer Programming Club, I've supervised volunteer teams, tracked attendance and participation, managed communication channels, and helped execute everything from inter-department competitions to national robofest sub-committees. It turns out event logistics and software architecture are the same discipline wearing different clothes: figure out what actually needs to happen, then make sure it happens.

I care about software that works the first time and reads clean the tenth. Small functions, honest names, predictable behaviour when someone pokes at it. I'd rather ship something boring and correct than something clever and fragile — and I'd rather tell you I don't know than guess at an answer.

Right now I'm deepening the fundamentals: REST API design, real-time system architecture, UI/UX principles, and the cloud deployment basics that turn a localhost project into something a stranger can actually use. I'm open to internships, junior developer roles, freelance builds, and collaborations where the problem is interesting and the people are straightforward.

If any of that sounds like a fit, the contact details are a few sections down — and I answer quickly.
